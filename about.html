About Sean's Transportation Service Inc.

A Georgetown-based transportation company serving businesses, organisations and individuals across Guyana with professional drivers, dependable vehicles and round-the-clock coordination.

Who We Are

Transportation You Can Plan Around

Sean's Transportation Service Inc. (STS) provides passenger transportation, vehicle rentals, cargo movement and relocation services from our base at Lot 34 First Street, Alexander Village, Georgetown.

Our clients include companies moving staff and crews, organisations hosting visitors and events, and individuals who simply need to be somewhere on time. Whatever the trip, the standard is the same: a professional driver, a clean and well-maintained vehicle, and a team that answers the phone.

Working With Us

From Request to Arrival

1. Tell us the trip

Send a quotation request online, by phone or on WhatsApp with the date, route, passengers or cargo and the vehicle you have in mind.

2. Confirm the quotation

We reply with a clear price and the vehicle assigned. Confirm, and the booking goes to the Command Centre schedule.

3. Your driver arrives

A professional driver arrives on time in a clean, air-conditioned vehicle. Active trips are monitored until you reach your destination.
